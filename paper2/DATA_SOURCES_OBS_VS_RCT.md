## PRIMARY DATA SOURCES

### 1. **Ioannidis 2001 JAMA Dataset** ⭐ HIGH PRIORITY

**Reference**: Ioannidis JPA, Haidich AB, Pappa M, et al. Comparison of evidence of treatment effects in randomized and nonrandomized studies. JAMA. 2001;286(7):821-830.

**Content**:
- **45 topics** comparing obs vs RCT estimates
- Covers diverse clinical areas
- Includes pooled estimates, confidence intervals
- Published in supplementary materials

**How to Access**:
```
1. Download from JAMA: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/194074
2. Supplementary eTable contains all 45 comparisons with data
3. Extract: Obs effect, Obs CI, RCT effect, RCT CI, sample sizes
```

**Expected Yield**: **45 cases** (high quality, peer-reviewed)

**Status**: PUBLIC DATA, immediately accessible

---

### 2. **Anglemyer 2014 Cochrane Review** ⭐ HIGH PRIORITY

**Reference**: Anglemyer A, Horvath HT, Bero L. Healthcare outcomes assessed with observational study designs compared with those assessed in randomized trials. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2014;(4):MR000034.

**Content**:
- Systematic review of **obs vs RCT** comparisons
- 16 specific interventions analyzed
- Includes meta-analysis of observational studies separately from RCTs
- Effect size differences quantified

**How to Access**:
```
1. Cochrane Library: https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.MR000034.pub2
2. Download supplementary data tables
3. Extract individual comparisons
```

**Expected Yield**: **16 cases** (high quality)

**Status**: PUBLIC DATA, free access via Cochrane
